# DropRun 0.5.12-rc.1 — clearer Share confirmation and honest feedback

Public candidate, October 6, 2026 UTC. The downloadable App now includes the
Share confirmation group, direct project-to-note transition, continuous reversed
scrim and History notice feedback. [Candidate 11](0.5.11-rc.1.md) remains immutable
history. Stable launch and marketing acceptance remain open.

## What changed

- Share keeps the complete destination and received material together on one
  matte surface before the optional note. Choosing an already allowed project
  advances directly to the note; the overlay continues from its current color
  when reversed. Existing permission, material and execution rules remain.
- History static status and retry notices no longer imply an action through
  clicking, keyboard focus or press feedback. Read failures retain their details
  action; changing to a static notice resets the previous press scale.
- Retention reading now has a bounded development native check. Policy values,
  requests and cleanup behavior are unchanged; this is validation, not a new
  retention capability. README and website examples retain their sample markers.
