GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
up front.

Does it work while my computer is off?+

The Relay can hold accepted handoffs while your computer is offline. Execution waits for the Connector to come online. This is not a cloud-hosted coding service.

Can it understand every video link?+

No. Access depends on the source. DropRun reports the text, audio, and frames it actually obtained. A link or cover image is never presented as a fully watched video.

What can I preview on my phone?+

Reports, screenshots, and files are the baseline. Supported static sites can have versioned snapshots. Live backend previews need your own domain and managed Cloudflare Tunnel.

Do I need a DropRun subscription?+

No official hosted service or subscription is included. You run your own Relay and use your own Codex account. Provider limits and usage charges still apply.

Is this a stable release?+

This is a public preview. Release notes list completed checks and remaining real-device, source-access, and reliability acceptance. Interface demos do not establish that those gates have passed.
