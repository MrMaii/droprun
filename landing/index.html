Before you drop in.

Does it run when my computer is off?+

Your Relay can hold accepted handoffs while the computer is offline. Execution resumes when your Connector is online. This is not cloud-hosted coding.

Can it understand every video link?+

No. Source access varies. DropRun reports the text, audio and frames it actually obtained. A link or cover image is never presented as a fully watched video.

What can I preview on my phone?+

Reports, screenshots and files are the baseline. Supported static sites can have versioned snapshots. Live backend previews require your own domain and managed Cloudflare Tunnel.

Is there a paid DropRun subscription?+

No official hosted service or subscription is included. You operate your own Relay and use your own Codex account. Provider limits and charges can still apply.

Is this already a stable release?+

This is the public preview line. Release notes identify completed tests and remaining device, source-access and reliability validation. No success rate is claimed without evidence.
